Privacy

School data stays private

Diana uses student information to provide study support, never targeted advertising.

Scope

This notice explains how Diana handles personal information on the public website and in a Diana account. Diana is the operator referred to as "we," "us," or "our." A school or district may provide an additional notice when it offers Diana to students.

Public website

  • The landing page can be viewed without entering a name, email, homework, grade, diagnosis, or school record.
  • A browser setting may remember a motion preference on the device.
  • Limited interaction events may record an action and its page location. Standard hosting logs may include an IP address, browser, device, request time, and referring page.

Account information

When an account is used, Diana may process the following information:

  • Account and age information: email address, password through the authentication provider, optional display name, date of birth, time zone, and account settings.
  • School and study information: classes, assignments, due dates, grades or feedback a student enters or imports, notes, flashcards, study plans, progress, mastery, authorship records, and learning preferences.
  • Files and media: documents, photos, PDFs, audio, voice recordings, transcripts, and other material a student chooses to upload.
  • AI activity: prompts, responses, feature used, model information, token counts, feedback, and safety or authorship logs.
  • Optional sensitive information: accommodations, learning challenges, sleep logs, wellness goals, and check-ins a student chooses to provide.
  • Connections and sharing: connected school-service details, imported course material, private share links, notification settings, and delivery information.
  • Security and technical information: sign-in records, device and browser details, error records, abuse-prevention signals, and service performance data.

Why we use it

Diana uses personal information to create and secure an account, organize schoolwork, provide coaching and study tools, personalize pacing and accessibility, connect requested school services, deliver notifications, support sharing chosen by the student, prevent abuse, troubleshoot problems, and meet legal obligations.

Diana limits collection and use to what is reasonably needed for these purposes. A new use that is materially different will require an updated notice and, when required, consent.

Student data promises

  • Diana does not sell student personal information.
  • Diana does not use student information for targeted advertising or build advertising profiles.
  • Diana does not use student schoolwork or sensitive information to train a general-purpose AI model.
  • Diana does not disclose student records to a school, parent, or other person unless the student chooses to share, a school agreement authorizes the access, or law and safety require it.

Service providers

Diana uses service providers only to operate requested features. The current technology stack includes Vercel for hosting, Supabase for authentication, databases, and file storage, OpenAI for AI and transcription features, ElevenLabs for enabled voice features, and email or push-notification providers when those features are turned on. Canvas and Google Classroom information is processed only when a student or school connects that service.

Providers may receive the information needed to perform their service. Contracts and settings must restrict them from using student information for their own advertising or model training.

When information is shared

Diana may disclose information to service providers, to a school or person the student has authorized, to protect a person or the service from serious harm, to comply with valid legal process, or as part of a business transaction that preserves the protections in this notice. Diana does not publish a student's work by default.

Health and wellness

Sleep and wellness features are optional. This information is treated as sensitive, is used only to provide the feature and protect the service, and is not sold or used for advertising. Diana is not a medical provider, and this information is not a medical record or a substitute for professional care.

Age

Diana accounts are for people age 13 or older. Diana asks for a date of birth to apply the age gate and blocks account creation for a person who reports being under 13. A person under the age of legal majority must have permission from a parent or legal guardian to use the account service unless a school agreement and applicable law provide another valid basis.

Schools and FERPA

FERPA applies directly to eligible schools, not automatically to every consumer app. When a school provides education records to Diana under the school-official exception, a written agreement must place Diana under the school's direct control for those records, limit use to the authorized educational purpose, restrict redisclosure, provide access and correction support, and require deletion when the information is no longer needed.

Retention and deletion

Diana keeps information only as long as reasonably needed for the service, safety, legal, and school-contract purposes described here. The current account-deletion workflow turns off AI access immediately and schedules account-owned database records for deletion after a 30-day recovery period.

A deletion request is not complete until stored files, authentication records, applicable provider records, and covered backups have also been deleted or isolated under a documented legal exception. Limited records may be retained when required for security, fraud prevention, legal compliance, or proof that a request was completed.

Your controls and rights

The Diana privacy dashboard lets an account holder download a copy of covered account information, clear supported data categories, manage sharing and notifications, and request account deletion. Information can also be corrected through the relevant account feature. A school-provided user may make a request through the school.

Depending on where a person lives, additional rights may include access, correction, deletion, restriction, portability, withdrawal of consent, an appeal, and freedom from discrimination for using a privacy right. Diana will verify a request before acting on it.

Security

Diana uses access controls, encrypted network connections, database row-level security, restricted service credentials, activity logging, and other safeguards designed for student information. No online service can promise perfect security. Diana will investigate suspected incidents and provide legally required notices.

Questions and requests

Account holders can use the Data and Privacy area inside Diana. A student, parent, or eligible student using a school-provided account may also contact the school that provided access. The operator's verified legal name, mailing address, and public privacy contact must be displayed here before general public launch.

Bottom line

Diana uses student information to provide the service, not to advertise to students or sell their data.

Terms

Your work stays yours

Diana can coach and organize. The student remains responsible for the work and every final choice.

Agreement

These terms apply to the Diana public website and account service. By creating an account or using the account service, a user agrees to these terms and the Privacy notice. A school-provided account may also be governed by a written school agreement. Mandatory law and the school agreement control if they conflict with these terms.

Age and permission

A Diana account holder must be at least 13. A person under the age of legal majority confirms that a parent or legal guardian has permitted the account unless a school agreement and applicable law provide another valid basis. Do not provide false age or identity information.

Accounts

Keep account credentials private and provide accurate information. Tell Diana or the school that provided the account if access may be unauthorized. A user is responsible for activity under the account unless applicable law says otherwise.

Academic integrity

Use Diana for coaching, organization, practice, revision, and study support. Do not use Diana to cheat, impersonate another person, fabricate sources, evade school rules, or submit AI output as original work when that is not allowed. The student must review any work and confirm any submission or sharing action.

Acceptable use

Do not misuse Diana to harass, threaten, exploit, or harm a person; create or distribute illegal or sexually exploitative material; obtain dangerous instructions; invade privacy; bypass safeguards; probe security without written permission; disrupt the service; scrape at scale; or infringe another person's rights.

Student content

A student keeps ownership of schoolwork and other content the student creates. The student gives Diana limited permission to host, process, reproduce, and transmit that content only as needed to provide, secure, support, and legally operate the service. This permission ends when the content is deleted, subject to limited backup, security, and legal retention.

AI limits

AI output can be incomplete, inaccurate, or inappropriate. Diana is a study tool, not a teacher, school official, medical provider, therapist, lawyer, or emergency service. Verify important facts, sources, calculations, deadlines, and school requirements with a qualified person or original source.

Connected services

A user may choose to connect a service such as Canvas or Google Classroom. The connected service has its own terms and privacy practices. The user authorizes Diana to access and use connected information only to provide the requested feature and may disconnect it subject to the provider's controls.

Illustrative screens on the public site are not a guarantee that every feature is available in every account. Material changes to these terms will be presented before they apply when notice or renewed agreement is required.

Suspension and deletion

Diana may restrict or suspend access when reasonably necessary to protect a student, another person, a school, or the service; respond to unlawful use; or enforce these terms. When practical, Diana will provide notice and a way to ask for review. An account holder may request deletion through the account privacy dashboard.

Diana's software, branding, designs, and original service materials are protected by intellectual-property law. These terms allow personal use of the service but do not transfer ownership or permit copying, resale, reverse engineering where prohibited by law, or use of Diana branding to imply endorsement.

Disclaimers

To the extent allowed by law, Diana is provided "as is" and "as available." Diana does not promise a particular grade, school result, uninterrupted service, or error-free AI output. Nothing in these terms excludes a warranty or consumer right that cannot legally be excluded.

Applicable law

These terms are governed by applicable law. They do not require a student or consumer to give up a non-waivable right or remedy. The operator's verified legal name, principal location, and any governing-law or dispute terms must be supplied before general public launch.

Bottom line

Diana supports learning. It does not replace the student's judgment, school rules, or responsibility for the work.

Safety

Help keeps you in control

Diana guides the next step while the student keeps authorship, privacy choices, and the final say.

Core boundaries

  • Diana should coach and scaffold rather than replace the student's work.
  • A student must confirm submission, sharing, and other consequential actions.
  • AI access can be limited by class or assignment rules.
  • AI interactions and authorship events may be logged to support transparency and safety.

AI safety

Diana uses feature restrictions, safety instructions, access controls, and activity records designed for students. These controls reduce risk but cannot catch every inaccurate, biased, unsafe, or inappropriate response. A student should stop and ask a trusted adult when a response feels unsafe, confusing, or wrong.

Academic safety

Diana should help a student understand, practice, plan, and revise. It should not impersonate the student, fabricate evidence, hide AI use, defeat a teacher's instructions, or complete prohibited work. School and teacher rules always matter.

Privacy and sharing

Do not enter information that is not needed for the study task. Review a private share link before sending it, share only with the intended person, and remove access when it is no longer needed. Never post another person's school record, contact details, private messages, health information, or image without permission.

Wellness features

Sleep and wellness tools are optional planning aids. They do not diagnose, treat, or monitor a medical or mental-health condition. Students should not rely on Diana for health decisions and should speak with a parent, guardian, school professional, or qualified provider when support is needed.

Prohibited conduct

Do not use Diana for harassment, threats, sexual exploitation, self-harm encouragement, dangerous instructions, discrimination, stalking, impersonation, privacy invasion, illegal activity, security attacks, or attempts to bypass age, school, or safety controls.

Schools and adults

Access by a school, teacher, parent, guardian, or other adult must follow the student's sharing choice, a valid school agreement, or applicable law. Diana should not promise that every adult can see everything or that every activity is invisible. The Privacy notice explains the allowed disclosures.

Reports and enforcement

Diana may preserve relevant records, restrict a feature, suspend an account, notify an authorized school contact, or contact appropriate authorities when reasonably necessary to address serious misuse, a credible safety risk, or a legal duty. Reports should be reviewed by a trained person, with an appeal path when appropriate.

Not an emergency service

Diana is not an emergency, crisis, medical, mental-health, or legal service. If someone may be in immediate danger, stop using Diana and contact a trusted adult or local emergency services. Do not wait for an app response.

Bottom line

Diana should help a student move forward without taking over the work, the student's privacy choices, or the final decision.

Accessibility

Learning should stay usable

Diana includes adjustable reading and motion settings so students can shape the experience without changing their schoolwork.

Available preferences

  • Students can adjust text size, line spacing, reading font, and letter and word spacing.
  • Reading support includes bionic reading, line focus, and optional visual pacing.
  • Read-aloud controls offer voice, speed, and pitch preferences where supported.
  • Students can choose reduced motion and higher-contrast display preferences.

Getting support

Accessibility preferences are available in account settings. If a student cannot use part of Diana or needs help with a specific access need, contact support@diana.app.

Bottom line

Diana should adapt to the student, not make the student adapt to the tool.
