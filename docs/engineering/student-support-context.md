# Student-selected tutor support

The Lobby check-in includes optional food, water and break choices. A student can save or clear a choice independently of the existing wellness check-in. A choice is eligible for use for 24 hours and remains stored until it is cleared, replaced or the account is deleted. It is included in student data exports and can also be cleared from privacy controls. Diana does not ask for meal details, calories or medical information here.

Sharing support guidance with AI is a separate, explicit preference and defaults off. When enabled, recognized accommodation choices, a recent mood and an unexpired daily support choice can become fixed instructional guidance. The provider receives general guidance, not stored diagnoses, free-form IEP text, the mood value, the specific daily choice or its timestamp. Students may independently include personal information in text or files they submit for help. Turning this preference off stops the new support-context sharing on subsequent requests; it does not cancel an already dispatched request or delete the stored choice or information the student includes in an AI request.

Tutor support remains bounded by owner and assignment authorization, the stored class AI policy, source evidence and student authorship. Explicit requests for more explanation and recorded help signals can increase structure. The system does not claim to diagnose, detect every instance of difficulty, understand every subject, or establish learning gains. Educator review, prospective evaluation and subject-specific validation remain necessary.

The database migration must be applied before releasing the Next application and updated Edge Functions. Without the migration the new controls and personalization are unavailable. Roll out with opt-in disabled by default, verify owner isolation, consent, expiration and clearing, and verify deployed function source against the release. Existing course/provider and learning-feature gates are unchanged.
