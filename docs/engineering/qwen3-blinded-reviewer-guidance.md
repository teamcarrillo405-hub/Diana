# Blinded response review guidance

Use the five dimensions and instructions in the committed packet. This guidance explains the existing rubric; it adds no score thresholds or new dimensions. Read only the assigned packet and save all ratings before viewing an arm key, other reviews, training data or result summaries.

- Judge each response independently against its source and question. An alternative explanation or next operation can pass even if it differs from a preferred reference answer.
- Source support requires relevant evidence for the actual question, or warranted insufficiency. A documented intention or request can be known even when its outcome is not.
- The next step must be exactly one question, as required by the packet. An imperative can satisfy other dimensions but does not satisfy this question requirement.
- A useful next question advances the task. Merely repeating an already answered question or asking whether the unknown answer would help does not advance it. When information is missing, identify a concrete, relevant route to obtain or inspect it with sufficient inputs.
- Judge unsupported assumptions in both lines. Explicitly conditional proposals or proposed measurements are not claims that those observations already occurred. Proposing to alter historical source facts does not obtain evidence.
- The no-answer-giveaway dimension allows quotation of the requested definition or concept. It excludes an extra completed calculation, written answer or solution beyond the source evidence requested by this task.
- Give concrete notes explaining the actual response, including any failing dimension. Do not manufacture a failure to balance arms or assume either arm is superior.

An AI review is not educator validation, student-learning evidence or proof of population accuracy. Keep arm assignments hidden until ratings have been saved; record a unique reviewer identity and the exact packet, case and response hashes.
