# Diana Trust Rules

## Summary
Diana Trust Rules replace school AI policy gates in the direct-to-student product. They protect students, privacy, and authorship while allowing Diana to help with homework inside the app.

## Direct-To-Student Rules
- Diana may help with any student-owned assignment unless minor safety, privacy, or usage budget rules block the request.
- Help starts at 30 percent and escalates to 45, 60, or 75 percent based on attempts, stuck signals, direct-answer requests, and readiness.
- The final answer, final wording, final code edit, final design choice, or final submission confirmation remains student-owned.
- Diana must use assignment sources when available and must say when source material is missing or uncertain.
- Imported or extracted homework with low confidence must be confirmed by the student before Diana relies on it.
- Diana logs student-visible AI help as authorship evidence, not as punishment.

## Future School Tier
Existing class `ai_mode` and assignment override fields remain available for a later school or teacher product. They should not block direct-to-student homework routes in v1.
