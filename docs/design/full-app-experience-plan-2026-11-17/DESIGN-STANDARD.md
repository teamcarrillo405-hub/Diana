# Approved campus design standard

Approved by the owner on November 18, 2026, with one correction: the backgrounds were too dark. The brighter scene treatment is the resulting implementation.

- **Direction:** a confident college-football menu atmosphere without XP, streaks, ranks, trophies or competition mechanics.
- **Identity:** the Diana athletic wordmark, Campus Slab navigation, Saira Condensed display type and paintbrush selections. Readable body text uses Lexend or the student's saved reading preferences.
- **Personalization:** two school colors and school short name plus Campus. The uploaded school logo belongs on the Lobby banner and its setup preview.
- **Backgrounds:** recognizable daylight, architecture and room detail. Reduce broad dark overlays rather than brightening the source image. Retain localized shading only where exposed text needs it.
- **Work:** calm opaque reading, writing, graph and form surfaces. Task clarity and saved accessibility preferences take priority inside the workspace.
- **Motion:** short, subtle transitions; respect reduced-motion preferences. Ambient video is a later enhancement rather than a requirement for this release.
- **Phone:** the approved compact navigation, readable text and reachable next action. Retain all five primary destinations and at least 44px touch targets.
- **Consistency:** the approved Lobby, Work, Classes, Calendar, Quiz and Settings compositions are the references for future pages. Keep their rooms distinct.
