## Honest Completion

Judge the rendered result rather than the engine name or exported pixel count.
A fixed-camera composite can be convincing; flat planes do not gain physical
human lighting merely by moving them into Three.js. Do not add fake camera
parallax, bloom or sharpening to disguise source limitations. Record any genuine
source-quality blocker instead of repeatedly declaring another layout pass AAA.
