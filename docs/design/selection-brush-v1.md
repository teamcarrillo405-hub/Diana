# Today Selection Brush

- Generator: built-in `image_gen` tool, one generated raster asset.
- Project asset: `public/assets/today-now/selection-brush-v1.webp`.
- Generated dimensions: 2172 x 724, transparent RGBA PNG.
- Web delivery: 1024 x 341 WebP, 81,884 bytes, alpha preserved.
- Integration: a luminance mask tinted with the existing pink selection color. Only the preview category indicator uses it. Tab dimensions, focus outlines, keyboard behavior, and authored motion timing remain unchanged.

## Generation Prompt

Create ONE production-ready raster texture asset for a premium sports-video-game menu selection marker. This is a brush-stroke mask, NOT a UI mockup. Canvas: extra-wide 3:1 landscape, ideally 1536 x 512. A single horizontal swipe of thick WHITE acrylic paint laid left-to-right by a real broad, flat bristle brush, photographed perfectly head-on with flat even light, isolated on a genuinely TRANSPARENT background. Stroke occupies approximately 96% of canvas width and 90% of canvas height; minimal transparent padding. Realistic irregular bristle tips at both ends, individual fine parallel bristle drag lines along the top and bottom margins, tiny dry-brush pinholes mainly near the perimeter, slightly imperfect hand-painted boundaries and a few delicate short stray paint hairs attached to the edge. The central 80% of the stroke must be densely opaque white and visually calm because a readable menu label will be placed over it later. Make the left end fuller, softly ragged and loaded with paint; the right end finishes in a natural combed taper but stays broad, not a pointed triangle. Upper/lower contours are gently uneven, NOT jagged geometric polygons. Natural variation in pigment opacity around the edges. The result must read immediately as a REAL PHYSICAL BRUSH STROKE at small display size. NO text, NO letters, NO numbers, NO logo, NO brush tool, NO hands, NO splatter clouds, NO multiple strokes, NO border, NO rectangular panel, NO shadows, NO perspective, NO paper or fabric background, NO checkerboard printed into the asset, NO glow. White paint and transparent alpha only.
